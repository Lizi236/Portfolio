IoT Hydroponic Monitoring System
May – Aug 2026
Software development intern, NJ Pathways to Agribusiness Internship

Built a hydroponic monitoring system from the hardware up. I programmed an ESP32 microcontroller to read live environmental and water-quality sensors and send data wirelessly over MQTT, then configured a Raspberry Pi as a local server running a full pipeline — MQTT, Node-RED, InfluxDB, and Grafana — to process, store, and visualize the readings on live dashboards. I also wired, soldered, and calibrated the hardware myself, pH and EC sensors included.
